# Evidence and regulatory context

## Prototype thresholds

The CPCB-published textile-effluent table lists pH 6.5–8.5 and inorganic TDS 2,100 mg/L for treated effluent, with notes covering disposal mode, intake-water TDS, reuse, CETP responsibility, and the possibility of stricter board requirements. ChromaGuard uses these values as prototype references—not as universal legal clearance.

- CPCB: [Standards for discharge of effluents from textile industry](https://cpcb.nic.in/displaypdf.php?id=SW5kdXN0cnktU3BlY2lmaWMtU3RhbmRhcmRzL0VmZmx1ZW50LzQxMi5wZGY%3D)
- CPCB: [Common Effluent Treatment Plant standards](https://cpcb.nic.in/displaypdf.php?id=SW5kdXN0cnktU3BlY2lmaWMtU3RhbmRhcmRzL0VmZmx1ZW50L0NFVFAucGRm)

Turbidity and the TCS3200-derived colour-intensity bands are simulation parameters and are not represented as statutory values.

## Tiruppur context

The source deck uses historical figures of 700+ dyeing/bleaching units and 18 operating CETPs. A TNPCB policy note records 754 units covered by closure proceedings and 18 operating textile CETPs in the 2011–2014 context. These are useful problem-history figures, not confirmed current market counts.

- TNPCB: [Policy Note 2013–2014](https://tnpcb.gov.in/PDF/About_Us/PolicyNote/PolicyNoteEnglish2013-2014.pdf)

A more recent TNPCB CEPI report states that dyeing and bleaching units in its study area recover 98% of effluent for reuse and that industrial effluent discharge to water bodies is not permitted. It separately reports major untreated domestic sewage discharge. For that reason, this repository does not repeat the deck's approximately 96 MLD figure as current industrial textile discharge.

- TNPCB: [Tiruppur CEPI evaluation and action plan](https://tnpcb.gov.in/PDF/About_Us/projects/CEPI/CEPI_TprRpt4920.pdf)
- TNPCB: [Continuous Noyyal and Kalingarayan water-quality monitoring](https://tnpcb.gov.in/waterquality.php)

## Interpretation rule

Before a field pilot, replace prototype thresholds and historical context with site-specific consent conditions, current TNPCB/CPCB directions, calibrated samples, and qualified process-safety review.
