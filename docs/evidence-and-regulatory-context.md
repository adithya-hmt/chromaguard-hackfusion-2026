# Evidence and regulatory context

## Prototype thresholds

The CPCB textile-effluent table lists pH 6.5 to 8.5 and inorganic TDS at 2,100 mg/L for treated effluent. Its notes cover disposal mode, intake-water TDS, reuse, CETP responsibility, and stricter local requirements. ChromaGuard uses the values as prototype settings. They do not authorize discharge.

- CPCB: [Standards for discharge of effluents from textile industry](https://cpcb.nic.in/displaypdf.php?id=SW5kdXN0cnktU3BlY2lmaWMtU3RhbmRhcmRzL0VmZmx1ZW50LzQxMi5wZGY%3D)
- CPCB: [Common Effluent Treatment Plant standards](https://cpcb.nic.in/displaypdf.php?id=SW5kdXN0cnktU3BlY2lmaWMtU3RhbmRhcmRzL0VmZmx1ZW50L0NFVFAucGRm)

Turbidity and the TCS3200-derived colour-intensity bands are simulation parameters and are not represented as statutory values.

## Tiruppur context

The submission uses historical figures of more than 700 dyeing and bleaching units and 18 operating CETPs. A TNPCB policy note records 754 units covered by closure proceedings and 18 operating textile CETPs between 2011 and 2014. These figures describe the earlier problem; they are not current market counts.

- TNPCB: [Policy Note 2013–2014](https://tnpcb.gov.in/PDF/About_Us/PolicyNote/PolicyNoteEnglish2013-2014.pdf)

A more recent TNPCB CEPI report states that dyeing and bleaching units in its study area recover 98% of effluent for reuse and that industrial effluent discharge to water bodies is not permitted. It separately reports major untreated domestic sewage discharge. For that reason, this repository does not present approximately 96 MLD as current industrial textile discharge.

- TNPCB: [Tiruppur CEPI evaluation and action plan](https://tnpcb.gov.in/PDF/About_Us/projects/CEPI/CEPI_TprRpt4920.pdf)
- TNPCB: [Continuous Noyyal and Kalingarayan water-quality monitoring](https://tnpcb.gov.in/waterquality.php)

## Interpretation rule

Before a field pilot, replace prototype thresholds and historical context with site-specific consent conditions, current TNPCB/CPCB directions, calibrated samples, and qualified process-safety review.
