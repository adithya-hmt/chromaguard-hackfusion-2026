# Classification logic

`COMPLIANT` means all inputs are present and inside prototype bands. `WARNING` means one non-critical warning. `NON_COMPLIANT` means a critical pH/TDS/turbidity/colour rule or at least two warnings. `SENSOR_FAULT` means missing, non-finite, or implausible input. Limits are configurable prototype defaults, not universal legal limits. Validate against applicable TNPCB/CPCB requirements and field samples before use.
